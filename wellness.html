A few gentle practices

Find your steady ground.

Try one of these simple approaches when you need a little space to reset.

01 ♡

Give your feelings a name

Try describing what you're feeling without judging it. Naming an emotion can help you understand what might be happening.

Reflect in your journal ↗
02 ✳

Try 5–4–3–2–1 grounding

Slowly notice 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell and 1 you taste. Take your time with each step.

A sensory grounding practice
03 ❋

Make space around stress

Choose one manageable task, take a short break or reach out to someone you trust. You don't need to solve everything at once.
