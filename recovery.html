You do not have to do this alone

Support from a trained professional, counsellor, doctor, or trusted person can make a meaningful difference.

Asking for help is a strength. You deserve support without judgement.

Explore support and helplines →

Support is always worth reaching for.

If you are looking for help with substance use or addiction, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional or a de-addiction service.

India's National De-addiction Helpline: 1800-11-0031

You can also contact Tele-MANAS at 14416 for mental health support.

If you are in immediate danger, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Ashraya is a wellbeing support resource, not a substitute for professional treatment. Recovery can involve setbacks and different kinds of support. A difficult day does not erase the effort you have made.
