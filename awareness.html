01
Self-awareness

Understanding your mind

Sometimes we experience an emotion before we understand what caused it. Paying attention to our thoughts, feelings and physical responses can help us notice patterns without judging ourselves.

Try asking yourself:

  • What emotion am I experiencing right now?
  • Has something happened that might have affected my mood?
  • What would help me feel supported in this moment?

You don't need to find an answer to everything. Sometimes simply noticing what you feel is a meaningful first step.

02
Open conversations

Breaking the stigma

Misunderstandings about mental health can make people feel isolated or ashamed. Mental health challenges are not a personal failure, and asking for help is not a sign of weakness.

How can we make a difference?

  • Listen without judging or immediately offering advice.
  • Avoid labels and assumptions about someone's experience.
  • Respect privacy and personal boundaries.
  • Encourage professional support when someone needs it.

A little empathy can make a conversation feel safer. We don't always need the perfect words; sometimes listening is enough.

03
Everyday care

Small ways to care for yourself

Self-care isn't about being productive or following a perfect routine. It can be as simple as recognising that you need rest, connection or a little time away from a stressful situation.

Some gentle ideas to try:

  • Step outside for a few minutes and notice your surroundings.
  • Write down a thought you've been carrying.
  • Reach out to someone you trust.
  • Take a break and do one thing at a comfortable pace.
  • Try a grounding exercise if you feel overwhelmed.

Choose what feels manageable today. It is okay if your version of self-care looks different tomorrow.
